# Afro Violin — Brand Bible v1 (instrumental compilation channel)

> Locked by Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor. YouTube-only, like Divine Freedom Soapbox. The style is what Nnamdi calls "Afro violin."

## What it is
A **compilation channel**: the family's best songs from all the *other* accounts, re-rendered as **instrumental versions in the Afro-violin style** — the track gets a slightly Afrobeat/pop feel, and the vocal melody is carried by a **violin instead of a voice.**

It already holds a version of their best work so far, and it grows automatically with every future song from every account.

## The rule
Every vocal single → a matching **Afro-violin instrumental** on this channel. One song = two products (the vocal on its home account + the instrumental here).

## The style ("Afro violin")
- Slightly Afrobeat / Afro-pop groove.
- The lead **violin replaces the vocal line**.
- Warm, melodic, premium — classical elegance × West-African rhythm (this is the locked `afroviolin_v1` DNA).

## Distribution
- YouTube only (primary).
- The instrumental versions are a discovery/volume play — people who search "afro violin instrumental / focus / study music" find this.
