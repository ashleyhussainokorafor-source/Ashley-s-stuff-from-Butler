# Divine Freedom Soapbox — Brand Bible v1

> Locked by Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor. (Earlier listing said "Diving" — it's **Divine**.) YouTube-only channel.

## What it is
A spiritual/religious music channel. The method: **take a sacred or spiritual text, extract its teaching, and turn it into a song.** Then keep doing that across as many traditions as possible.

## Source texts (so far, and expanding)
- **Upanishads** (Vedic / Hindu philosophy)
- **The Ethiopian Bible** (Ethiopian Orthodox canon — includes Enoch, Jubilees, etc.)
- **The Emerald Tablets of Thoth** (Hermetic / esoteric)
- …and the stated plan is to reach as many religious + spiritual texts as possible.

## Tone & position
- **Reverent, universal, philosophical.** Draws wisdom from many traditions, not preaching a single one — it's the meditation on the *teaching itself*, across faiths.
- Respectful of each tradition's language and imagery; never mockery, never Syncretism-as-a-gimmick.
- The voice of a seeker collecting timeless wisdom in song.

## Content pillars
1. The teaching (the verse / wisdom itself)
2. The song (music born from that teaching)
3. The visual (that tradition's aesthetic — Vedic, Ethiopian iconography, Hermetic symbols, etc.)
4. Context / "what this text actually says" (light educational framing)

## Goal
**More content per song → more plays.** Each song should spawn multiple content forms rather than living as a single upload (see repurposing playbook).

## Distribution
- YouTube only (primary). Cross-posting can be decided later.

## Repurposing playbook (to expand later in the plan)
Each sacred song ideally becomes: full music video · 3–5 Shorts (clips) · a spoken "the teaching" reel · quote-card images · an ambient/long-play mix · grouped into a tradition-based playlist (Vedic, Hermetic, Ethiopian, etc.).
