# Freedom Soapbox Collective — Brand Bible v1

> Locked by Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor. The **overflow / catch-all** account of FS Records.

## What it is
An account that distributes good songs that don't cleanly fit any of the other artist accounts — still great work, just uncategorized. Sort of a "generic dump" for the excess of quality music the family produces.

## Positioning
- The **miscellaneous release valve** — no shame to it, it's where good-but-orphan tracks get a home.
- Lower curation bar than Freedom Soapbox (the flagship family story), higher freedom than any single artist identity.

## Content
- Any track that's genuinely good but doesn't belong to Peoploids / NVA FRQCY / APX Shft / Afro Violin / the special family canon.

## Future
- As the catalog grows, tracks here may graduate INTO a proper artist or a themed compilation. The Collective is the holding pen, not a graveyard.
