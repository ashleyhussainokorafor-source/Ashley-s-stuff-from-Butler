Freedom Soapbox started as a **family YouTube channel** — a homemade vlog dump + an aspiring podcast that never fully became one. It got made into a "family brand" — a bit messy, no real direction — but that's its origin and its soul.

**It is NOT primarily a monetized asset. It is the living story of the family's journey.**

## The vision for it
- **Clean it up** and give it real direction.
- Turn content that's been **sitting in drives and devices** into real vlogs.
- Give the family's **home movies a place to live** — so they can enjoy watching themselves grow.
- It's the documentary of Nnamdi, Ashley, Amara, and Anaya — the biopic of a family in motion.

This account does NOT post just any song. It only carries **special songs** — ones about the family, their journey, their beliefs and values. It's **the soundtrack to the family's living journey.**

Each of those special songs deserves a **masterpiece music video** — not a routine drop. These are flagships.
