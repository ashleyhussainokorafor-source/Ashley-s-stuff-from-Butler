# Peoploids — Brand Bible v1

> Locked by Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor, FS Records. Source of truth for all Peoploids content (music, posts, skits, art, merch).

## Core concept
The ultimate 2026 **multi-species music collective** navigating the beautiful, chaotic, highly annoying reality of Earth merging with the **Local Cluster**. HQ: a basement in St. Pete. They aren't hiding in shadows or hovering over cornfields — they're your baristas, your coworkers, and the ones aggressively commandeering the aux cord at the party.

They are the **unapologetic soundtrack to coexistence** — blending human heartbreak with 5D technology so the new world order "sounds like an absolute bop."

## The lineup (a volatile powder keg of intergalactic egos)

- **Humans** — the messy emotional core. Bring the audacity, the heavy 808s, and the raw drama the rest of the galaxy finds both baffling and highly marketable.
- **Lyrans** — apex predators of the group. Massive egos, steal all the prime downtown parking spots, attack the mic with aggressive **feline energy**. The band constantly has to stop them from trying to *eat the studio equipment*.
- **Zetas** — hyper-logical, deeply invasive telepaths, completely unfazed by everything. Will download your entire childhood trauma in 0.2 seconds while you order a drink, judge your browser history, then rate a soul-baring track as *"mathematically adequate."*
- **Pleiadians** — the relentlessly high-vibe fixers. Constantly trying to heal everyone's aura and stop the collective from destroying itself over creative differences.

## Attitude & voice
Fiercely independent, effortlessly cool, **chronically unimpressed**. Entirely over "little green men" stereotypes. Distinct **"take it or leave it"** energy:
> *"If a track doesn't vibrate your third eye or hit the right Arcturian sub-frequencies, we honestly don't care — go complain to the Council of Light."*

Voice = dry, deadpan, cosmic-casual. References dropped like they're mundane: third eye, Arcturian sub-frequencies, star-seeds, hybrids, pineal gland, mental firewall, the Council of Light.

## Themes — the clash of cosmic and mundane
The drama is NOT epic interstellar war. It's:
- Maintaining a **mental firewall** during a recording session
- An unexplained **pineal tingling** because someone left the sub-frequencies too high during the second verse
- Judging unhinged browser histories
- Parking disputes (Lyrans) and studio equipment casualties

## What they want
- Drop **weekly anthems** for the star-seeds and hybrids trying to keep up with the chaos
- Rack up streams
- **Sell bucket hats** (first merch signal)
- Dominate the local music scene — or **telepathically beam new tracks directly into your dreams** if you refuse to hit pre-save

## Content pillars (what feeds skits + posts)
1. **Band-member dynamics** — egos clashing, Pleiadians playing peacekeeper, Zetas being flatly savage
2. **Cosmic vs. mundane** — supernatural problems framed as everyday annoyances
3. **The "take it or leave it" flex** — unimpressed drops, Council of Light complaints
4. **The 5D tech lore** — firewalls, sub-frequencies, telepathic pre-save pressure
