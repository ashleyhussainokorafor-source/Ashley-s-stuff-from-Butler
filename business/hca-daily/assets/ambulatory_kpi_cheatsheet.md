## 1. Patient Access & Scheduling Benchmarks

| Metric | Industry Standard / Target | Why It Matters | How to Cite in an Interview |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Third Next Available Appointment (TNAA)** | **Primary Care:** &lt; 5–7 days<br>**Specialty Care:** &lt; 14 days | Standardized measure of real patient access without same-day cancellation distortion. | *"Reduced specialty TNAA from 24 days to 11 days by standardizing 30-minute block templates."* |
| **No-Show & Late Cancellation Rate** | **Target:** &lt; 6.0%–8.0%<br>**Red Flag:** &gt; 12.0% | Measures appointment leakage and lost provider clinical capacity. | *"Lowered clinic no-show rate from 13.8% to 6.4% via two-way conversational SMS workflows."* |
| **Provider Schedule Utilization Rate** | **Target:** 88%–92% of booked slots completed | Evaluates whether clinical schedule blocks are translating into encounters. | *"Maintained 91% schedule utilization across 8 providers by managing dynamic waitlist backfills."* |
| **Door-to-Doctor Wait Time** | **Target:** &lt; 15–20 minutes | Direct predictor of patient satisfaction percentiles and clinic throughput efficiency. | *"Trimmed door-to-doctor wait times by 14 minutes through digital pre-registration."* |

## 2. Physician Productivity & Work RVU (wRVU) Ranges

*Work Relative Value Units (wRVUs) measure clinical intensity and volume independent of commercial payer fee schedules.*

| Specialty Area | MGMA 25th Percentile | MGMA 50th Percentile (Median) | MGMA 75th Percentile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Family Medicine (Outpatient Only)** | ~4,200 wRVUs/yr | **~4,900 wRVUs/yr** (~19–20/day) | ~5,800 wRVUs/yr |
| **Internal Medicine (General)** | ~4,300 wRVUs/yr | **~5,100 wRVUs/yr** (~20–21/day) | ~6,100 wRVUs/yr |
| **Pediatrics (General Outpatient)** | ~4,100 wRVUs/yr | **~4,800 wRVUs/yr** (~19/day) | ~5,600 wRVUs/yr |
| **Cardiology (Non-Invasive)** | ~6,800 wRVUs/yr | **~8,200 wRVUs/yr** | ~10,100 wRVUs/yr |
| **Orthopedic Surgery (General)** | ~7,400 wRVUs/yr | **~9,100 wRVUs/yr** | ~11,400 wRVUs/yr |
| **General Surgery** | ~6,100 wRVUs/yr | **~7,500 wRVUs/yr** | ~9,200 wRVUs/yr |

### Operational Takeaway:
* When managing a provider whose productivity is below the 25th percentile, do not tell them to "work faster." 
* Audit their **Medical Assistant ratio** (MGMA benchmark: 1.5 to 2.0 MAs per provider) and examine documentation lag time in the EMR.

## 3. Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) & Front-Desk Quality

| RCM Metric | Top Performer Benchmark | Poor Performer / Action Trigger | Operational Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clean Claim Rate** | **&gt; 96.0%** | &lt; 90.0% | Implement front-end eligibility clearinghouse scrubbers prior to check-in. |
| **Initial Denial Rate** | **&lt; 4.0%** | &gt; 8.0% | Root-cause analysis on top 5 denial reason codes (CO-4, CO-16, CO-197). |
| **Days in A/R (Accounts Receivable)** | **&lt; 32–36 days** | &gt; 50 days | Accelerated follow-up on unworked claims over 60 days. |
| **Front-Desk Point-of-Service (POS) Collections** | **&gt; 85%** of copays/deductibles collected at check-in | &lt; 60% | Mandatory pre-visit patient balance notification & card-on-file protocol. |

## 4. Labor Economics & Staffing Ratios

* **Staffing Benchmark:** 3.5 to 4.5 non-provider FTEs per Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) Physician in primary care (Front desk: 1.0, MA/LPN: 1.5–2.0, Billing/Admin support: 1.0).
* **Premium Overtime Ceiling:** Overtime hours should never exceed **2.0% to 2.5%** of total productive labor hours in a well-run ambulatory center.
