# The HCA Daily — Brand Guide

The single source of truth for how **The HCA Daily** looks, sounds, and positions
itself. Reference this before building any website page, logo, product, or asset.

> Approved by Ashley (Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor, DBA) — Sep 2026.

---

## Brand essence

**Authoritative, warm, and a little funny — the professor who tells you the truth.**

Our competitors are sterile and corporate (heavy navy, "credentialed institution" energy).
We win by being *approachable authority*: doctor-level credibility (DBA) delivered with
direct, warm, human energy. Trust us because of the credential, like us because of the voice.

## Color palette (exact — use these values everywhere)

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | `#0b2545` | Primary. Headers, buttons, trust elements. The "authority" base. |
| **Navy 2** | `#13315c` | Gradients, hovers, secondary panels. |
| **Teal** | `#0d9488` | Healthcare accent. Links, highlights, "medical" cues. |
| **Teal 2** | `#0f766e` | Hover states on teal. |
| **Gold** | `#c9a227` | Warmth + CTAs. The "personality" pop. Use sparingly for maximum effect (buttons, key accents). |
| **Ink** | `#14181f` | Body text. |
| **Muted** | `#5b6572` | Secondary text, captions. |
| **Background** | `#f7f9fb` | Page background. Light, clean, not stark white. |
| **Line** | `#e4e9f0` | Borders, dividers. |

**Rule:** Navy is the base, teal is the healthcare signal, **gold is the personality** — use
gold for the things we want people to *feel* (CTAs, "get the vault") and never wallpaper a
page with it. Competitors lean navy-but-sterile; our gold is the differentiator.

## Logo direction (when it's created)

- **A clean wordmark** — "**The HCA Daily**" (or "Dr. Ashley Hussain"), set in a modern,
  confident sans-serif, navy.
- **Mark:** a subtle healthcare signal (caduceus / plus / EKG line) in **teal**, with a small
  **gold** accent. Keep it minimal and ownable — *not* a busy seal.
- **Feel:** authoritative (like a credential) but approachable (like a mentor), not corporate.
- One-color versions must work: navy-on-white, and white/gold-on-navy for dark backgrounds.

---

## Voice & tone

- **Direct, warm, plain-spoken.** No corporate filler, no "we help healthcare organizations
  synergize." Talk like a smart professor, not a hospital press release.
- **Lead with specifics** — real metrics, real numbers ("no-show 40%→19%"), never vague promises.
- **Warmth is on-brand.** A little humor / "Katt Williams energy" is fine sparingly — it's the
  thing competitors can't copy.
- **Authority first, never apology:** "Dr. Ashley, DBA — former lecturer who taught the people
  who get hired." Lead with the credential, then be human.

- **Kimberly Cook / Healthcare Admin Launchpad (Blue Pen Resumes):** $5,000 / 8-week high-touch
  coaching, MPH-led, "schedule a call" everywhere. Navy/blue corporate.

- **Leland / 1:1 coaches:** $249/hr, bespoke.
- **TMG / Barlow-McCarthy:** clinician burnout/leadership EQ — adjacent, not job-search.
- **Free content mills** (Indeed, universities): generic, shallow interview Q&A.

**The gap:** nobody does productized + automated + affordable career help. That's us.
