## Email 2: Day 1 (Value Teardown: The Task Trap)

* **Subject:** Why 90% of healthcare admin resumes get screened out
* **Preview Text:** Stop listing duties. Start quantifying operational outcomes.

> Hi [First Name],
> 
> Here is a hard truth about healthcare leadership hiring:
> 
> **Executives don't hire people who attend meetings. They hire people who protect clinical operations and bottom-line margins.**
> 
> Most resumes we review look like this:
> * *“Responsible for overseeing clinic scheduling and patient flow.”*
> * *“Attended weekly throughput and patient safety committee meetings.”*
> * *“Monitored department expenses and supply ordering.”*
> 
> None of those bullet points tell the hiring committee if you actually *succeeded* or simply occupied a chair.
> 
> ### The 3-Part Operational Formula
> Whenever you describe your experience, use this structure:  
> `[Strong Action Verb] + [Specific Operational Bottleneck] + [Quantified Metric Outcome]`
> 
> Compare:
> * **Weak:** *"Managed physician appointment templates."*
> * **Executive Grade:** *"Restructured provider scheduling blocks across a 6-physician clinic, decreasing third-next-available appointment wait times by 14 days and recapturing $112,000 in lost encounter capacity."*
> 
> Look at your current resume today. Pick **one** bullet point and run it through that formula.

## Email 3: Day 3 (Industry Literacy: Executive KPIs)

* **Subject:** The 4 metrics every healthcare leader must speak
* **Preview Text:** How to sound like a director before you have the title.

> Hi [First Name],
> 
> The fastest way to stand out in a screening interview with a VP of Operations, Chief Medical Officer, or CFO is speaking their language.
> 
> If you only talk about *"great teamwork"* and *"improving morale,"* you sound like an entry-level applicant.  
> If you talk about these four operational levers, you sound like a peer:
> 
> 1. **Provider wRVUs vs. MGMA Median:**  
> Work Relative Value Units measure actual physician productivity regardless of billed charges. Understand how template changes directly impact provider wRVU generation.
> 
> 2. **Third Next Available Appointment (TNAA):**  
> The gold standard for measuring patient access without being skewed by same-day cancellations.
> 
> 3. **FTE Labor Variance:**  
> The difference between budgeted productive hours and actual hours worked. Knowing how to mitigate contract/traveler nursing spend while maintaining safety ratios is the #1 skill hospital leadership looks for.
> 
> 4. **Clean Claim Rate & Denial Lag:**  
> Operations and Revenue Cycle (RCM) are inseparable. If your front desk makes registration errors, cash flow stalls 90 days later.
> 
> Keep this email handy. When you prepare for your next career step, these metrics are your compass.
>
