### From 2026 hook research (State of Hooks 2026 + Shorts retention studies)

- **Self-introductions are the single worst opener of the year** — "Hi guys, today I'm going to…" scored **~18 points below** average
- **Curiosity-gap openings outperform generic intros by ~12%** on first-3-second retention
- **Specific numbers beat round numbers by ~9 hook-score points**
- **Pattern interrupts under 2.5s lift retention by ~+9**
- **~1/3 of first-time viewers watch MUTED** → the *visual* must carry the hook alone
- Retention by length: **15s → 85–95%** · 30s → 70–82% · 60s → 50–65% (cliff at 0:42)
- Shorts and Reels **punish audio-only openings** (unlike TikTok)
- **Kinetic text on the beat + burned-in captions** — never rely on YouTube's default captions
- **One CTA**, never three

## Production spec (every Short)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Length | **15–25s** (target 85%+ retention) |
| Frame 1 | Pattern interrupt + curiosity gap. **No greeting, no logo, no intro.** |
| Text | Kinetic, on-beat, burned in. Must read **muted**. |
| Captions | Burned-in, glanceable. Never YouTube default. |
| Numbers | Specific and **true** — never invented |
| Colors | Navy base · Teal signal · **Gold CTA** |
| CTA | Exactly one, at the end |
| Sign-off | "Dr. Ashley, DBA" — authority, warm, brief |

**Hard rule: never invent a statistic.** If we don't have a real number, use a real question instead.

## Filming guidance (for Ashley)

- **Film vertically, eye-level, phone is fine.** Talking head beats animation — your brand *is* you.
- Shoot **all five in one sitting** (~20 min). Same shirt, same light, same spot = consistent channel signal.
- **Open mid-thought.** Never "hey guys." Cut the first second off every take in editing.
- Burn in captions in the brand palette.
- Keep one hand free to gesture — it reads as energy on mute.
- Warmth: one dry, wry beat per script. The "professor who tells you the truth," not a lecturer.

**Why this wins:** these are the exact questions your audience already searches, in the format
your own channel proved works, edited to the retention rules that decide Shorts distribution.
