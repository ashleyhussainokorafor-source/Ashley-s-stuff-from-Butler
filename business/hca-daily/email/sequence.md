Hi {{first_name}},

Give me 90 seconds and I'll give you a framework that works in any healthcare-interview room.

Every strong behavioral answer has **three parts**:

1. **A baseline** — the number you started with. ("We were running a 40% no-show rate.")
2. **The action** — what *you* specifically did. ("I rebuilt the reminder protocol and moved to same-day open scheduling.")
3. **The result** — the number you ended with, plus a benchmark. ("No-shows dropped to 19% — the MGMA 50th percentile.")

That's it. Baseline → action → result. Most candidates give you #2 and skip the numbers on either side. That's the whole game.
