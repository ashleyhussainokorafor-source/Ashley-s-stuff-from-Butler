# What Your Viewers Are Asking — HCA Daily YouTube Audit

**Channel:** The HCA Daily with Professor Ashley (6,190 subs, 253 videos)
**Method:** pulled comment threads from the 60 most recent uploads via the YouTube Data API
**Sample:** 40,699 views · 136 comments harvested · **50 of them questions or explicit requests**
**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Raw data:** `ops/viewer_questions.json`

---

## 1. The headline: it's one question, over and over

**30 of the 50 questions (60%) are about choosing a degree.** MHA vs MPH vs MBA vs MPA vs DHA.

This isn't a content idea. It's the single question your audience has, and they're asking it
in the comments of videos that don't answer it:

- *"Please make a video on MPA vs MHA"* (2022-12-23)
- *"Hai professor make a video on MPA vs MHA THANKYOU"* (2022-12-23) — **requested twice, same day**
- *"Which is best MHA or MPH for bright future and carrier?"*
- *"Currently obtaining my MHA from Widener University. Is it worth it to pursue a Doctorate?"*
- *"Clinical social worker here +18 years. Many of my peers went the dual degree MSW/MPH but i'm thinking to just get MBA HCare Admin. Thoughts?"*
- *"can a mba in healthcare and hospital management help a person to become CEO, COOs in hospital and healthcare care sector?"*
- *"MPH vs health informatics in US for usmle?"*

**The MPA vs MHA video has been requested twice and never made.** Your existing *MHA vs MPH*
video is one of your best performers (3,674 views, 15 comments). The demand is proven and unmet.

---

## 2. The finding that matters most: you have never replied. Not once.

**Channel replies in the harvested set: 0.**

136 comments. People asking direct questions about their careers, their money, their lives.
Zero responses from the channel.

That's the cheapest growth lever you have, and it's completely unused. Every one of those
50 questioners is a warm human who wanted to hear from you and got silence.

It also has a business cost: three of them asked to **buy** something and got no answer (§5).

## 3. Your audience is not who you think it is

The comments are dominated by **international career-changers**, not US healthcare insiders:

| Commenter | Their situation |
|---|---|
| India | *"persuing ba English and literature degree in India. can i take mha as my master's degree? ... I don't have any healthcare background"* |
| Brazil | *"change career of being a dentist (in Brazil) and go for a Master of Healthcare Administration"* |
| Hong Kong | *"working as a clerk in Hong Kong public hospital for 7 years, and recently planning to immigrate to UK"* |
| Algeria | *"the website doest work :( , iam connecting from Algeria ? is that à problém?"* |
| India | *"BTech CSE passed out but couldn't get a job its been 2 years... planning to get a job in hospital sector"* |
| India | *"bsc nursing completed I was confused to take which course after"* |
| Nigeria/US | *"MBBS to HCA in the US"* |
| General | *"Does this apply to just US ? how about aussie , UK Canada....."* |

They are trying to **enter a US healthcare career from outside it** — often from outside the
country. Your content reads to them as the guidebook for a door they can't get through.

**This is a positioning decision, not a content decision.** Either you serve the global
career-changer (huge audience, lower willingness to pay, needs visa/credentialing content), or
you serve the US insider (smaller, higher intent, higher willingness to pay). Right now your
content speaks to the second while your audience is the first.

## 4. Zero-experience entry is the #2 pain — and your products don't solve it

9 of 50 questions are some version of *"I have the degree and no experience, how do I get in?"*

- *"How can i enter the Healthcare Administration job with master's degree... with no experience or 0 exp with only qualification in my hand."*
- *"I graduated with an MHA degree in April. It has been hard to secure a job, any advice?"*
- *"Im actually 40 years old... worked in a medical office on the clinical side for 17 years. I'm now a undergrad student"*
- *"recently graduated with a Bachelor of Science with a concentration in healthcare and minor in Psychology, what is a good entry level job for experience?"*

**Your products are a $27 Interview Answer Vault and a $297 Career Accelerator — resume and
interview tools.** Your audience's stated problem is *getting the interview in the first place*.
That's an upstream gap: you're selling the answer to a question they haven't reached yet.

## Recommended next moves, in order of payoff

1. **Reply to all 50 questions.** Free, immediate, and no one has done it yet. Start with the
   three buying-intent comments — those are revenue, not engagement.
2. **Make the MPA vs MHA video.** Requested twice, never made, and degree choice is 60% of all
   questions. Cheapest possible content win.
3. **Decide the audience question.** Global career-changer or US insider. Everything else
   follows from this, including pricing.
4. **Answer the "no experience" gap.** Either a lead magnet or a product — it's the #2 pain and
   nothing you sell addresses it.
5. **Test the site from outside the US.** Algeria report is unverified.
6. **Tell commenters your products exist.** Two people described the Vault in their own words.

---

## Method notes / limits

- Sample is the 60 most recent uploads, not the full 253-video catalog. Older high-traffic
  videos may hold additional signal.
- Replies were harvested one level deep; deep reply chains may be missed.
- Theme tags overlap — one comment can count in several themes.
- Comment counts come from the API's `commentCount`, which includes replies.
