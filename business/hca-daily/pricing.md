# The HCA Daily — Product Pricing & Monetization Strategy

**Approved by CEO:** September 2026  
**Status:** Active  

---

## Product Suite & Pricing Matrix

### 1. Free Community + Resources
* **Price:** Free forever ($0)
* **Target:** Students, recent graduates, career pivoters exploring healthcare administration.
* **Core Value:** Top-of-funnel acquisition, community networking, brand authority.
* **Included:**
  * Online community networking hub
  * Downloadable starter kit: entry-level HCA resume template, basic salary overview
  * Weekly HCA newsletter with industry trends, market news, and community spotlights

---

### 2. HCA Career Navigator (Primary Revenue — Recurring SaaS)
* **Target Audience:** Active job seekers, early-to-mid career HCA professionals seeking advancement or pivot into management.
* **Pricing:**
  * **Monthly:** $29 / month
  * **Annual:** $199 / year (~$16.50/month; 43% savings)
* **Capabilities & Features:**
  * Unlimited AI Career Advisor sessions tailored specifically to HCA paths
  * Role-specific resume and LinkedIn optimization (Practice Management, HIM, Revenue Cycle, Hospital Ops)
  * Real-time salary benchmarking and negotiation playbook
  * Certification roadmaps (FACHE, CPC, CPPS, PMP)
  * Curated weekly job market alerts and skill gap analysis

---

### 3. HCA Interview Coach (Secondary Revenue — Usage-Based Add-on)
* **Target Audience:** Candidates preparing for high-stakes screening or panel interviews.
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Session:** $19 (1 complete role-specific interview + real-time STAR feedback & scorecard)
  * **Interview Prep Pack:** $49 (3 sessions + follow-up drilling & readiness score)
* **Subscriber Perks:**
  * Annual Navigator members: 1 free session per month
  * Monthly Navigator members: 20% discount on all packs ($39.20)

---

## Key Revenue Metrics to Track
* Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate (Target: 3–5%)
* Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
* Annual Plan Mix (Target: 30%+ annual commitment)
* Interview Coach Attach Rate to Navigator Subscribers (Target: 15–20%)
