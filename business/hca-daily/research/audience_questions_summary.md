# What @professorashley's audience is actually asking

**Research date:** 2026-09-18
**Method:** 372 comments mined from the 40 most-viewed videos (130 of them questions),
cross-referenced against YouTube search autocomplete (what people type whether or not
they've found the channel). Raw data: `research/audience_questions.json`.

Two independent signals. Where they agree, the topic is real demand *and* real intent.

---

## The ranked findings

### 1. "What jobs can I actually get with this degree?" — STRONGEST SIGNAL
Both signals agree, repeatedly.

- *"What jobs can we get after the degree..other than being an assistant. Please guide me... I'm very perplexed"* — **20 likes** on the 85,664-view video
- *"What kind of work can you do with a health science degree, like if you don't pursue further education and just have that bachelor degree?"* — **10 likes**
- *"What would be some entry level positions one could apply for if they have chosen the HCA path?"* — 2 likes
- *"Can you do video please of what jobs someone can get with a masters in public administration"* — 4 likes
- Autocomplete: `healthcare administration jobs`, `healthcare administration degree jobs`,
  `healthcare administration entry level jobs`

**Read:** the single most repeated anxiety. People are finishing degrees and don't know
what the degree maps to. The word "perplexed" is doing a lot of work.

### 2. "What's the stress level / what's the day actually like?" — HIGHEST AGREEMENT
- *"What about the stress level?"* — **22 likes**, the single most-liked question in the whole set
- Autocomplete: `healthcare administration day in the life`

**Read:** emotional/realistic question, not informational. She already owns the
"What does an HCA actually do?" topic (55,390 views) — stress is the natural sequel.

### 3. "Healthcare Management vs Healthcare Administration" — PROVEN + STILL ASKED
- *"Which one is better health care management or heath care administration??"* — 6 likes
- *"Is there a difference between healthcare management and administration?"* — 3 likes
- *"What about healthcare management degree?"* — 4 likes
- Autocomplete: `healthcare management vs healthcare administration`

**Read:** her existing video on this has **49,406 views**. It keeps getting asked anyway,
which means the old video isn't answering it clearly enough for new viewers.

### 4. "Is it too late for me? (career changer / older)" — HIGH EMOTION
- *"I'm 55, starting my entire career over or, my 2nd/3rd career... I have no experience in the medical arts"* — **12 likes**
- *"Is it late to switch from a different profession to health care Administration in the late 30's? I need some motivation please."* — 3 likes
- Autocomplete: `healthcare administration career changer`

**Read:** these people explicitly ask for motivation. High comment/engagement potential.

### 5. "How do I get a mentor / network when I know nobody?"
- *"My issue now is how to get a mentor?"* — 2 likes
- *"I am in school for healthcare management online, but I don't know anyone in that health field. How would you suggest I go about..."* — 2 likes

**Read:** she has a video on this already but it has only **362 views** — the topic is
underserved on her channel relative to the demand. Online-degree students have no network
and know it.

### 6. "I'm clinical (radiology / MD / medical assistant) — can I cross over?"
- *"I'm majoring in radiology and have never studied any non medical subjects like finance HR marketing etc. is it still possible"* — **8 likes**
- *"Can u start out as a Medical Assistant while u are getting your bachelor's in Healthcare Administration?"* — 4 likes
- *"I am an MD graduate, how much will I be preferred in MHA?"* — 2 likes

**Read:** a distinct, underserved segment. Related to the nurse question but NOT the same —
these are people already inside healthcare, not outsiders asking whether to get in.

### 7. "How do I actually apply for jobs well?" — HIGH AGREEMENT
- *"Would love to see more content on better ways to apply for jobs!"* — **18 likes**

**Read:** direct request for content, second-most-liked. Ties straight to the paid products.

### 8. "What software / tools should I learn before starting?"
- *"Any suggestions which software to prepare yourself before you going to start this career?"* — 2 likes

**Read:** small but very concrete and highly actionable — good for a practical Short.

## Secondary signal: heavy international demand

Autocomplete for "hospital administration" returned almost entirely **Malayalam**
variants, plus strong India / Dubai / Germany / UK / Canada job queries:

`hospital administration jobs in dubai`, `healthcare management jobs in germany`,
`healthcare administration jobs in canada`

**Read:** real volume, but likely low commercial value for US-priced products ($27/$297).
Worth knowing before spending production effort chasing it.
