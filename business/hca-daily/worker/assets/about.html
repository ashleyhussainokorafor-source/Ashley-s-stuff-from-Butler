I taught this subject for six years. Then I watched my students graduate without knowing what their degree was for.

That question — "but what job does this actually get me?" — is the reason this site exists.

Dr. Ashley Hussain-Okorafor, DBA

The pattern I couldn't unsee

From 2016 to 2022 I was a full-time lecturer in healthcare administration at California State University, San Bernardino. Over those six years I taught healthcare delivery systems, operations, and organizational theory to more than 500 undergraduates, and I mentored a large share of them one-to-one on careers and graduate school.

Every single semester, the same thing happened. They'd do the work. They'd pass. They'd walk out with the degree — and then ask me the question they should never have had to ask: "What jobs can I actually get with this?"

They weren't confused because they hadn't studied. They were confused because the coursework taught them the field and nobody taught them the language the people doing the hiring were listening for.

"The candidates who get offers aren't the ones with the best résumés. They're the ones who sound like they already work there."

I'd been on the other side of the table too

Before and alongside teaching, I worked inside the system — as a Health Information Management specialist doing records audit and coding compliance, and in clinic operations and community health access work. So I knew what the job actually looks like when nobody's grading it.

My doctoral research looked at malpractice litigation involving nurses: how risk moves through a healthcare organization, and what administrative leadership can do about it before it reaches a courtroom. That work is where I learned to think in the numbers this field runs on — wRVUs, days in A/R, denial rates, no-show rates, cost per case.

What I built

In 2022 I started The HCA Daily. The idea was deliberately small: instead of another course, a five-minute daily drill — a streak you don't want to break, in the vocabulary that decides offers.

That grew into the free 90-second scorecard, the daily practice path, the Interview Answer Vault, and the AI tools that work through your actual situation with you. Along the way I started publishing on YouTube as @professorashley, where thousands of healthcare leaders have followed along.

Why it's structured the way it is

Most preparation material teaches you to answer questions. Very little of it teaches you to think the way the person across the table thinks.

So everything here is built around that gap — a short daily rep, a score that tells you where you actually stand, and depth only when you want it. Self-paced, automated, and it doesn't waste your time being motivational.
