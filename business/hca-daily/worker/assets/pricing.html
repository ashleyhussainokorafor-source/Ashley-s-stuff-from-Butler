Questions

Is this a subscription?

Only the Career Navigator. The Vault is $27 once, the Coach is $19 for one session or $49 for three, and the Accelerator is $297 once — those never renew. The Navigator is $29/month or $199/year and you can cancel anytime; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get what I paid for?

Immediately after checkout you land on a page with your access. The Vault and the Accelerator are PDFs — download them straight away and keep the file. The Navigator and the Coach unlock in your browser automatically, for 30 days at a time.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 30 days and we'll refund you — you keep the free playbook either way.

Do I need the free part first?

It helps. The scorecard tells you which of the four hiring dimensions is costing you offers, so you buy the depth you actually need.
