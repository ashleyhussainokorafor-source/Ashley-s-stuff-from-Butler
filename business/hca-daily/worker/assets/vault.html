The Problem

Generic interview advice loses jobs

Googling "healthcare administration interview questions" gets you the same 12 softballs every candidate reads. Weak answers like "I'd improve productivity" or "I'm a team player" don't get you hired — they get you forgotten.

Healthcare executives interview for metrics. They want to hear wRVUs, third-next-available, days in A/R, denial rates, HCAHPS composites. If you can't speak that language in the room, you lose to the candidate who can.

Hear the Difference

This is what a real answer sounds like

Q: "Tell me about a time you improved physician productivity."

"That provider's production rose from 3,800 to 4,750 wRVUs over the next twelve months — just under the 50th percentile — while the group's wRVU per FTE moved from 4,950 to 5,180."

Not "I'd push doctors to see more patients." A number. A benchmark. A result. That's what gets the offer.
